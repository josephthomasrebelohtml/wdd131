SPLASH White Water Rapids Trips

Learn more about the amazing trips offered by us and supported by our services! Whether this is your first time whitewater rafting, or you've been doing this for several years, we have trips prepared especially for you!

Descent to Detroit

  • 2 Hour Long Trip
  • Available June - September
  • $100 - $200 USD Per Person
  • Made for Intermediate to Advanced rafters, this short trip will take us all the way down to a beautiful spot in the borders of Detroit!

Down The Water Slide

  • 3 Hour Long Trip
  • Available April - October
  • $50 - $100 USD Per Person
  • Made for Beginners, this adventure is perfect to take a short and calm trip with the whole family!

Half Day Adventure

  • 8 Hour Long Trip
  • Available May - October
  • $150 - $250 USD Per Person
  • Made for Intermediate to Advanced rafters, we follow a long path taking several breaks in the way to rest and recuperate. Our final stop will be a beautiful waterfall!

Radiant Forest

  • 4 Hour Long Trip
  • Available April - August
  • $200 - $250 USD Per Person
  • Made for Intermediate rafters, this is for those that want to test their newfound skills and spend a few hours going down a mesmerizing forest!

Rocky Rapids

  • 7 Hour Long Trip
  • Available June - August
  • $250 - $300 USD Per Person
  • Made for Advanced rafters, this adventure follows a very rocky path and will test your abilities and endurance in white water rafting!

Mountain Descent

  • 10 Hour Long Trip
  • Available June - September
  • $300 - $400 USD Per Person
  • Made for Advanced to Expert rafters, this is for those who want a long adrenaline rush after many months of monotonous city life, or those who want to experience the hardest challenge Michigan has to offer!
